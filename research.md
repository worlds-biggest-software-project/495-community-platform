## Relevant Industry Standards or Protocols

- **ActivityPub (W3C)** — federated social networking protocol powering the Fediverse; increasingly relevant as communities seek data portability
- **OpenID Connect / OAuth 2.0** — standard for single sign-on and identity federation across community integrations
- **WCAG 2.1 AA** — web accessibility standard that enterprise and public-sector community operators must meet
- **GDPR / CCPA** — data privacy obligations governing member data, especially for paid membership platforms collecting payment information
- **Stripe Connect / PayPal Payouts** — de facto payment infrastructure for community monetisation and revenue sharing with sub-community operators

## Market Research

**Market Size:** The online community platform market is valued at approximately USD 1.4 billion in 2026 and is forecast to grow at a CAGR of over 20% through 2030, driven by creator economy expansion and enterprise internal community adoption.

**Funding:** Circle raised USD 25 million in 2022. Mighty Networks has raised approximately USD 50 million. The category continues to attract investment as brands seek owned-community alternatives to social media platforms.

**Pricing Landscape:** Entry-level plans range from USD 19–99/month. Growth-tier plans with full monetisation and analytics run USD 100–400/month. Enterprise plans with SSO, custom domains, and API access vary from USD 500–5,000+/month. Most platforms take no revenue share on member payments, positioning against marketplace-style alternatives.

**Key Buyer Personas:** Independent creators and coaches converting social media audiences into paid memberships; SaaS companies building customer success communities to reduce churn; professional associations replacing legacy forum software; brands seeking owned community alternatives to Facebook Groups.

**Notable Trends:** Community builders in 2026 are prioritising member outcomes and retention over raw growth metrics. Gamification (streaks, challenges, leaderboards) is now standard. Native live events within community platforms are replacing standalone webinar tools. The shift from ad-supported social media to subscription-based owned communities is accelerating.

## AI-Native Opportunity

- AI community moderator: automatically detects and handles spam, off-topic posts, and policy violations with configurable escalation to human moderators
- Personalised content surfacing: recommendation engine surfaces threads, events, and members most relevant to each member's interests and activity history
- Engagement forecasting: predicts which members are at risk of churn and triggers targeted re-engagement campaigns before they go quiet
- AI-assisted event planning: analyses member availability, topic preferences, and past attendance to recommend optimal event timing and formats
- Automated member onboarding journeys: AI sequences introductory content, connection suggestions, and milestone prompts based on each new member's profile and stated goals
